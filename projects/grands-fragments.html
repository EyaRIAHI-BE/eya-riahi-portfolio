Project Overview

This project focuses on the CAD modeling of a complete large-fragment orthopedic instrumentation set.

The instrumentation includes drilling, tapping, bending, measuring, extraction and screw-handling tools used during orthopedic surgical procedures.

The project includes the modeling of the individual instruments, their components and the overall instrumentation tray.
