Project Overview

This project involved the CAD modeling and technical development of a complete Grands Fragments orthopedic instrumentation set. More than 20 components were modeled and prepared for technical documentation.

The instrumentation includes drilling guides, taps, drills, countersinks, trephines, extractors, bending instruments, measuring tools, screwdrivers, aiming guides and sterilization equipment.
